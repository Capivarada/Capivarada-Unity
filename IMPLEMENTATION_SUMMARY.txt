================================================================================
  MATCH FLOW SEQUENCE
================================================================================

PHASE 1: INITIAL COUNTDOWN (10 seconds)
├── Title: "A partida vai começar em..."
├── State: RoundState.Countdown
├── Counter: 10, 9, 8...1
└── Event: OnTitleChanged("A partida vai começar em...")

PHASE 2: CHOOSING (8 seconds per round) [LOOP START]
├── Title: "Rodada {X}: Escolha a direção e a força"
├── State: RoundState.Choosing
├── Counter: 8, 7, 6...1
├── Players: All alive can choose
├── Human: Camera direction + input force
├── Bots: Auto-decide
└── Event: OnTitleChanged with round number

PHASE 3: REVEALING (DYNAMIC - NO FIXED TIMER)
├── Title: "Direções Reveladas!"
├── State: RoundState.Revealing
├── Wait: For all players to stop OR all eliminated
├── Check: velocity.magnitude < StopVelocityThreshold
├── Show: All indicators for alive players
├── Then: Hide indicators
└── Event: OnTitleChanged("Direções Reveladas!")

PHASE 4: LAUNCHING (INSTANT)
├── Title: (No title change)
├── State: RoundState.Launching
├── Action: Call Launch() on all alive players SIMULTANEOUSLY
├── Duration: Instant (no fixed timer)
└── Note: Bots do NOT re-decide during this phase

PHASE 5: MOVING (UNTIL STOP)
├── Title: (No title change)
├── State: RoundState.Moving
├── Wait: For all players to stop
├── Check: velocity.magnitude < StopVelocityThreshold
├── Delay: Use StopCheckDelay before confirming
├── Action: RegisterRoundSurvival() for alive players
└── Data: Add round survival to PlayerMatchData

PHASE 6: NEXT ROUND PAUSE (3 seconds) [IF CONDITIONS MET]
├── Condition: AlivePlayers > 1 AND CurrentRound < MaxRounds
├── Title: "Próxima rodada"
├── State: RoundState.NextRound
├── Counter: 3, 2, 1
├── Event: OnTitleChanged("Próxima rodada")
└── Then: Loop back to PHASE 2

END CONDITIONS:
├── Single Player: Match ends immediately
│   └── Winner gets Placement = 1
├── Max Rounds Reached: All alive marked as winners
│   └── Each: Placement = 1, Won = true
└── Final Event: OnMatchFinished with all winners

   └── Used in: RoundFlow() loop condition
   └── Default: 10 rounds
   └── Range: 1-100

5. StopVelocityThreshold
   └── Used in: WaitForAllPlayersToStop(), WaitForPlayersToStop()
   └── Default: 0.15 units/second
   └── Range: 0.01-0.5

6. StopCheckDelay
   └── Used in: WaitForPlayersToStop()
   └── Default: 0.75 seconds
   └── Range: 0.1-2 seconds

7. RevealingDuration (stored but NOT used - revealing is dynamic)
   └── Kept for compatibility
   └── Default: 1.5 seconds
